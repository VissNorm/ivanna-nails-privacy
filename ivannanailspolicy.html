Privacy Policy

Ivanna Nails Art SIA  ·  Last updated: 1 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ivanna Nails Art SIA ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores and protects personal information when you contact us, book an appointment, or interact with us through our website, telephone line, or social media channels including Instagram and Facebook. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Who we are

Ivanna Nails Art SIA is a nail care salon registered in Latvia, operating studios at Braslas iela 29, Riga, and Rūpniecības iela 44, Riga. For the personal data described in this policy, Ivanna Nails Art SIA acts as the data controller.

Information we collect

We collect only the information needed to book and manage your appointments and to communicate with you:

  • Your first and last name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address, if you provide it
  • The content of messages you send us by phone, Instagram, Facebook, or other channels
  • Appointment details such as the service requested, studio, preferred master, date and time

We do not collect payment card numbers, bank details, government identification numbers, health records, or biometric data through our booking channels.

How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Book, confirm, change and manage your appointments
  • Respond to your enquiries and communicate with you
  • Send appointment reminders and confirmations
  • Provide customer service and improve our services

Legal basis for processing

Under the GDPR, we process your personal data on the basis of your consent (which you give when you contact us to make a booking) and our legitimate interest in managing appointments and running our salon. Where we are required to keep records by law, we process data on the basis of our legal obligations.

How we share your information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it only with:

  • Service providers who help us operate our booking and communication systems, including scheduling and calendar tools and automated messaging assistants that handle enquiries on our behalf. These providers process data only on our instructions.
  • Authorities or third parties where we are required to do so by law.

When you contact us through Instagram or Facebook, the Meta Platforms also process your data according to their own privacy policy.

How long we keep your information

We keep your personal data only for as long as necessary to manage your appointments and our relationship with you, and to meet any legal record-keeping requirements. When data is no longer needed, we delete it securely.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restrict or object to our processing of your data
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw your consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Latvian Data State Inspectorate (Datu valsts inspekcija).

Cookies and tracking

Our website uses only essential cookies needed for the site to function. We do not use tracking technologies for advertising purposes.

Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

Children's privacy

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children without the consent of a parent or guardian.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
